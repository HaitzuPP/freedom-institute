Who this is for

Three readers decide whether this work was worth doing

Every grade and every index entry is written so that these three can make a decision with it.

Journalists and researchers

They need to know what a device gives up when it is inspected and what a provider hands over when it is asked, before the trip rather than after it.

Organisers and civil society

They coordinate in real time under conditions where group membership is the sensitive fact and the connection itself may be the evidence against them.

Households making an ordinary choice

They are picking between two services on a Sunday afternoon and deserve a straight answer on which one collects less, keeps it for a shorter time, and lets them take it back.
