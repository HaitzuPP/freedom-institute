UR builds the products; the Institute is the check on them

UR is a global institute for privacy and encryption products. Its network is contributed rather than rented: more than 80,000 members supply bandwidth that anonymises traffic for people who cannot route around their own government. UR would organise and fund the Freedom Institute, and would also sit inside its scope of assessment.

A contributed network changes who holds the risk

Commercial privacy infrastructure concentrates trust in one operator and one jurisdiction; a member-contributed network spreads it across the people using it. That design is the reason UR can serve places where a single corporate exit node would be blocked or seized within a week, and it is also the reason UR's own claims need testing by someone who does not report to UR.

The Institute publishes the test results either way. A finding against UR is the proof that the structure works.
